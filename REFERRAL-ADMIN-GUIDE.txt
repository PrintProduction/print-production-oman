PRINT PRODUCTION — MANUAL 5-FRIEND REFERRAL DISCOUNT
===================================================

This is a PUBLIC instruction document when uploaded to a public repository.
NEVER put any customer's phone numbers, screenshots or order history here.

THE OFFER
"Share with 5 Friends & Get 20% Off!"
Share the website with five DIFFERENT contacts, and send proof to Print Production
through private WhatsApp. Business approval is required.

WHAT THE CUSTOMER DOES
1. Click Get 20% Off on the homepage or near the house plate collection.
2. Copy their personal URL such as https://printproduction.github.io/print-production-oman/?ref=PP-XXXXXXXXXXXX
3. Send the personal link to five different WhatsApp contacts.
4. Take five screenshots, one screenshot per conversation, with the contact
   name/number and the sent referral link visible. They may hide unrelated messages.
5. Mark the number of screenshots prepared on the website (0/5 to 5/5).
   This count is SELF-REPORTED and does not verify any claims.
6. At 5/5, click Send Proof & Request My 20% Discount, open the chat and
   ATTACH ALL FIVE SCREENSHOTS in WhatsApp. Send the prepared message.

WHAT THE BUSINESS MUST DO (MANUALLY)
1. Receive the WhatsApp message and five screenshots for referral code PP-... .
2. Check there are five different contacts, each showing the relevant link sent.
3. Check your private records that the same contacts have not already been
   counted for this referral, and that the referral meets your published terms.
4. If approved, issue a UNIQUE, SINGLE-USE discount code privately in WhatsApp,
   e.g. PP20-[YOUR-PRIVATE-ORDER-ID]. NEVER issue approved codes in frontend JS.
5. Keep a PRIVATE spreadsheet or notebook (NOT in GitHub) with referral code,
   date, counted contacts, approved redemption code, eligible order, used/unused.
6. When customer orders: confirm eligible quoted price, apply 20%, and confirm
   discounted total before payment. Example: OMR 25.000 -> save 5.000 -> pay 20.000.
7. After applying the discount, MARK THE CODE USED in the private record.
   Do not accept a redeemed code a second time.

LIMITATIONS
- Screenshot evidence can be incomplete or modified; you must exercise judgement.
- GitHub Pages has no server to verify shares, store screenshots, count truly
  verified referrals, issue trusted coupons, or prevent repeat redemption.
- The website shows only a screenshot-preparation checklist. No automatic
  eligibility or qualification is asserted.
- Decide and publish your offer rules: start/end dates, eligible products,
  stacking with sales, minimum order, one redemption per customer, disputes.
- Personal details and screenshots must not be published in your public repo.
